Race Director's Kit

Broadcast Features

Everything you need for professional race broadcasting

01

Live Standings

View real-time race positions, car numbers, driver names, and camera assignments for all participants.

02

Custom Overlays

Session info, standings tower, weather forecast, track map, and driver info — fully customizable with move, color, and font options.

03

Game Panels

In-game overlays for instant access to race data. Toggle them directly from the application.

04

Auto Cam

Intelligent camera autopilot that automatically selects the most exciting car to follow based on multiple factors.

05

Event Tracking

Track penalties, incidents, track limits, overtakes, and other race events as they happen.

06

Vehicle Telemetry

Detailed vehicle overview, pitstop times, lap times, and telemetry data including throttle, brake, and steering inputs.

07

Replay Controls

Go back in time with +5, +15, +30 second jumps, custom time input, and playback speed controls.

08

Notification System

Real-time notifications for race winners, fastest laps, penalties, track limits, and incidents.

09

Keybindings & Presets

Full keybinding customization and configuration presets to match your workflow.

Driver's View

Driving Overlay Features

A focused in-car overlay that keeps race-critical data on screen

01

Live Telemetry

Throttle, brake, and steering traces with RPM shift lights, speed, and gear for the focused car.

02

Pit Stop Estimation

Live breakdown of driver swap, penalties, damage, tyres, fuel, and virtual energy, including the total stop time.

03

Weather Forecast

Track grip, rain and wetness, track and ambient temperature, wind, and the forecast for the rest of the session.

04

Damage Monitor

Aero, body, and suspension damage percentages with colour-coded severity so you know what to repair.

05

Track Map

Stylised circuit map with live car positions, the pit lane, and warning markers.

06

Custom Layout

Enable, move, and scale each panel independently. The overlay hides itself automatically outside real time.

Pricing

The application is free to use. Some features require a paid subscription (annual fee: €30).

Free Features

  • Standings
  • Camera control

Premium Features

  • Events (incidents, track limits, etc.)
  • Vehicle overview and telemetry
  • Replay and replay controls
  • Keybindings
  • Autocam
  • Overlay
Disclaimer: Due to inconsistencies in the game's REST API, the application may sometimes exhibit undesirable behavior (especially when loading a session). It is best to open the application when the session is loaded. In addition, the API (REST, WebSockets, shared memory) behaves differently depending on the type of game session (daily, endurance, hosted, weekend, etc).
